01 The brand

Not just a drink. An experience worth coming back for.

Indulgence should be an everyday affair. Thickletto was born from a love for rich flavours and high-quality ingredients — and a simple idea: make a drink worth remembering.

Every Thickletto is built on the brand's own Magic Master Milk — no ice, no generic ice cream, just a thicker, bolder base. Custom-built machinery is designed for texture and consistency, so the shake in your hand tastes like the one that built the craving.

“Cravings that hit again & again.” Thickletto's own words
  • Magic Master Milk
  • No ice
  • No ice cream
  • Custom-built machines
  • 500+ micron cups
  • 100% recyclable

Born for cravings. Thickletto pairs a thick-shake menu with coffees, served in premium packaging designed to be sturdy, safe and 100% recyclable.

02 Why Thickletto

No shortcuts. Just the thick stuff.

01 / 05 Magic Master Milk
01As described by Thickletto

No ice. No ice cream. Just pure magic.

Thickletto doesn't dilute the drink with ice or scoop in generic ice cream. The base is the brand's own Magic Master Milk — built for body, texture and a full-flavoured finish.

02As described by Thickletto

Crafted with custom-built machines.

The thick shakes are made on specialised machinery designed for consistency, texture and thickness in every sip — equipment the brand says is fast and seamless to train staff on.

03As described by Thickletto

Consistency you can count on.

Every shake, every brew — the same rich taste, the same Thickletto magic, every single time. A repeatable product is what turns a first sip into a habit.

04As described by Thickletto

Sustainable, not just sippable.

Thickletto states that its packaging is eco-friendly and made to minimise waste — so the serve feels as considered as the shake inside it.

05As described by Thickletto

Virgin, recyclable cups. 500+ micron.

Premium virgin plastic, 100% recyclable — a sturdy, safe way to serve every Thickletto sip. The cup is part of the brand experience, not an afterthought.

03 Franchise benefits

Built to make business feel good.

Five claims, stated clearly. Every point below reflects what Thickletto advertises on its franchise page — including the fine print on gross profit. Terms belong in the franchise agreement, not a landing page.

Thickletto advertises attractive profit margins of approximately 40% gross profit, paired with a simplified model that doesn't rely on skilled labour — making operations easier and more scalable.

Gross profit is revenue minus the direct cost of goods. It is not net profit: rent, salaries, utilities, marketing and other operating costs are not included. The ~40% figure is as stated by Thickletto, not independently verified here, and is not a guarantee of earnings. Review the commercial model and your own location with independent professional advice.
